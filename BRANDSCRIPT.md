# LevoAir BrandScript

StoryBrand BrandScript behind the site copy on `copy/brandscript-scale-positioning`. Every page section maps to one part below.

## Positioning

LevoAir is the flight operations partner a drone data platform or flight program hands sites to when its contract pilot roster can't keep up. The buyer already knows capture has to be repeatable; that's their product. What they lack is flight capacity that shows up on the day, flies their protocol and passes their QA without them recruiting, vetting and training another pilot. No platform is named. The copy keeps "directly or through the flight partner you already use" so the primes we fly for read us as a partner they can grow with, not a competitor.

## What platforms need from pilots (research, 2026-11-01)

- **TraceAir** ([Fly for us](https://traceair.net/company/drone-pilot)): Part 107, area mapping experience, DroneDeploy or DJI Ground Station, RTK aircraft and base station preferred, at least four batteries, $1M liability insurance, the same site on the same day every week, fly their protocol and upload right after the flight, no processing. Values accuracy, reliability, punctuality and clear communication.
- **DroneDeploy Flight Services** ([blog](https://www.dronedeploy.com/blog/flight-services)): pilot dispatched within a week, mission-specific test flights before customer work, QA review on every upload, same-day data, pilots lay ground control. Sells consistency and turnaround to construction and energy customers.
- **Zeitview** (pilot job postings): Part 107, statement of work with a shot list per job, standardized workflows, pilots verify data completeness before submitting, mostly 1099 pilots with their own gear.
- **Gap:** no public numbers on no-show or reflight rates. The pain below is inferred from what they screen for, not from published data.

## 1. Character (the customer)

The flight ops lead at a drone data platform, or the prime running a flight program, who has more sites sold than reliable pilots to fly them.

**Want:** every sold site flown on its day and accepted by QA, without growing a contract pilot roster.

## 2. Problem

- **External:** contract pilots miss days, fly off-spec and upload late. Every new market means recruiting again.
- **Internal:** their team spends the week rescheduling and chasing uploads instead of serving customers.
- **Philosophical:** the customer signed with the platform, so the platform answers for a pilot it doesn't control.
- **Villain:** the 1099 roster: one person, one drone, no backup.

## 3. Guide (LevoAir)

- **Empathy:** we fly inside platform programs today and know what their QA rejects.
- **Authority:** FAA Part 107, LAANC handled, flies the client's app and protocol. Credentials (insurance, RTK aircraft) and flight counts are added in `src/lib/proof.ts` once verified.

## 4. Plan

1. Send the spec (protocol, deliverables, sites).
2. We fly a test site for your QA team to grade.
3. Hand over the schedule (fixed flight days, uploads the same day).

**Agreement plan:** your protocol, your app, one contact, one invoice, directly or through your existing flight partner.

## 5. Call to action

- **Direct:** Book a Test Flight.
- **Transitional:** the Drone License and Registration guides.

## 6. Failure (stakes)

A missed or rejected flight is a gap in the customer's record with the platform's name on it, and another pilot to replace.

## 7. Success

Sold sites go on the schedule the week they're sold, and uploads clear QA the first time.

**Identity transformation:** from recruiting pilots to adding customers.

## One-liner

LevoAir flies capture missions for drone data platforms and flight programs. We fly your protocol in your app, upload the same day and take on new sites without you hiring another pilot.
