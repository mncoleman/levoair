# LevoAir BrandScript

StoryBrand BrandScript behind the site copy on `copy/brandscript-scale-positioning`. Every page section maps to one part below.

## Positioning

LevoAir is the operator that runs recurring drone capture at scale: every site flown the same way, data on a fixed cadence. The copy leads with the customer's pain and our solution and does not lean on equipment ownership. The copy speaks to companies running capture programs across many sites (data platforms, builders, prime contractors) without naming any of them. It names working "directly or inside an existing flight partner's program" so the primes we fly for read us as a partner they can grow with, not a competitor.

## 1. Character (the customer)

A company that needs site data captured on schedule, across many sites, and builds decisions on that data.

**Want:** every site captured on time, the same way, without staffing flights.

## 2. Problem

- **External:** one flight is easy to staff; dozens of sites on a weekly cadence is not.
- **Internal:** they end up managing pilots instead of delivering results.
- **Philosophical:** data you make decisions on shouldn't depend on who showed up that day.
- **Villain:** the patchwork pilot network. Different freelancers, different gear, different results.

## 3. Guide (LevoAir)

- **Empathy:** we've flown the jobs and know what breaks when a program grows.
- **Authority:** FAA Part 107, LAANC and waivers handled, repeatable flight plans, construction and mapping experience.

## 4. Plan

1. Scope the program (sites, cadence, deliverables).
2. We fly every site on schedule.
3. Data lands in your format, every visit.

**Agreement plan:** we work inside your standards and reporting, directly or alongside your flight partner.

## 5. Call to action

- **Direct:** Plan a Program.
- **Transitional:** the Drone License and Registration guides.

## 6. Failure (stakes)

Missed captures become gaps in the record you can't fly back and fill.

## 7. Success

Every site on cadence, flown the same way, so the team works from complete data.

**Identity transformation:** from managing pilots to running a program that scales.

## One-liner

LevoAir runs recurring drone capture for construction, mapping and inspection programs. We plan the flights, fly every site the same way and deliver the data on your cadence.
